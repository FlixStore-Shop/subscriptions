How can we help you?

Everything you need to know about purchasing, receiving, and using your digital subscriptions from Flixstore.

1 How do I receive my account?

Once you checkout via WhatsApp and complete the bKash payment, our team will manually verify your order. Your premium account credentials (Email & Password) will be sent directly to your WhatsApp within 15-30 minutes.

2 Is there a warranty?

Yes! We provide a full replacement warranty for the entire duration of your purchased validity (1 Month or 3 Months). If your account faces any issues, simply message us on WhatsApp and we will fix or replace it immediately.

3 What payment methods are accepted?

Currently, we accept payments exclusively through mobile banking for fast transactions in Bangladesh. You can pay using bKash, Nagad, or Rocket. The specific number will be provided during the WhatsApp checkout process.

4 Can I renew my existing account?

It depends on the service. For platforms like Netflix or Spotify, we typically provide a brand new premium account to ensure maximum stability and security. Please ask our support team during checkout if you require an upgrade on your personal email.
